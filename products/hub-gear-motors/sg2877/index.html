    SG-2877 3 Nm Hub Gear Motor¶

    Product Overview¶

    SG-2877 is a compact 99 mm hub gear motor using a planetary reduction structure. It is designed for AGV, AMR, mobile robots, rehabilitation platforms and compact wheel-drive systems that require moderate torque in a compact package.

    The standard catalog configuration uses Hall sensors. An external motor controller is required unless a controller is specifically included in the quotation.

    Product Identity¶

    Item Description
    Model SG-2877
    Product type Hub gear motor
    Transmission Planetary gearbox
    Housing material PA66
    Gear material POM
    Standard sensor Hall sensors

    Key Specifications¶

    Parameter Value
    Outer diameter 99 mm
    Product thickness 64 mm
    Overall length including shaft 117 mm
    Reduction ratio 9.75:1
    Rated voltage 24 VDC
    Rated power 45 W
    Rated current < 3 A
    No-load speed 140 rpm ±10%
    Rated speed 130 rpm ±10%
    Rated torque 3 Nm
    Peak torque 10 Nm
    Maximum efficiency > 70%
    Backlash < 1.5°
    Waterproof rating IP67
    Weight 780 ± 20 g

    Motor and Electrical Parameters¶

    Parameter Value
    Phase resistance, catalog notation 0.82 ±8% mΩ
    Phase inductance 0.75 ±8% mH
    Full-scale phase current 15 A max
    Rated bus current 7.5 A max
    Locked-rotor current < 8 A
    Rated motor load 0.3 Nm
    Hall angle 120°
    Motor structure 18 slots / 20 poles
    Insulation class Class B

    Peak Torque Condition¶

    Peak torque: 10 Nm

    Peak-torque duration depends on the controller current limit, battery voltage, duty cycle, cooling, installation and ambient conditions. Confirm the required peak duration before selection.

    Standard Configuration¶

    The standard catalog configuration includes:

    • BLDC motor
    • Planetary reduction mechanism
    • PA66 housing
    • Hall sensors
    • Output shaft and mounting structure shown in the approved drawing

    Unless explicitly included in the quotation, the following are not standard claims:

    • Integrated driver
    • Absolute encoder
    • CAN, RS485 or EtherCAT communication
    • Position or torque closed-loop control
    • Wheel, tire or vehicle-side mounting hardware

    Typical Applications¶

    • AGV and AMR wheel drives
    • Mobile service robots
    • Rehabilitation mobile platforms
    • Inspection robots
    • Compact transport mechanisms
    • Custom wheel-drive assemblies

    Engineering Documents¶

    STEP models, 2D drawings, cable definitions and detailed mounting documents are available after application review and inquiry.

    Please provide wheel diameter, vehicle weight, required speed, required gradeability, wheel quantity, operating voltage, duty cycle and estimated order quantity.
