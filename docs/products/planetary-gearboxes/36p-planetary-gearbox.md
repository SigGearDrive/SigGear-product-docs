## Motor Compatibility

The adapted-motor sheet identifies DC motors with a nominal voltage of **3–48 VDC** and power **below 150 W**. If the motor cannot meet the gearhead installation requirements, the motor and gearhead interface details need to be negotiated separately.

These are adapted-motor conditions, not an electrical voltage or power rating for the mechanical gearhead itself. Confirm the motor shaft, mounting interface, input speed, duty cycle and operating point for the selected gearhead ratio.

## Dimensions and Interface

The published drawing shows a 36 mm body diameter and stage-dependent gearhead lengths of 30.6, 40.9, 51.2 and 61.5 mm. Use the approved drawing for shaft geometry, mounting holes, input-interface details and tolerance notation.
