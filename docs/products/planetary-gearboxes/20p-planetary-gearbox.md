The published drawing shows a 20 mm body, a 12 mm output-shaft extension and stage-dependent gearhead lengths of 20.1, 24.9, 29.7 and 34.5 mm. Download the official **2D Drawing PDF** above for the complete shaft, mounting, input-interface and tolerance notation.
