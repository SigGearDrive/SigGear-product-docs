## Motor Compatibility

The adapted-motor sheet identifies DC motors with a nominal voltage of **3–36 VDC** and power **below 28 W**. If a motor cannot meet the gearhead installation requirements, details need to be negotiated separately.

These are adapted-motor conditions, not an electrical voltage or power rating for the mechanical gearhead itself. Confirm the motor shaft, mounting interface, input speed, duty cycle and operating point for the selected gearhead ratio.

## Dimensions and Interface

The published drawing shows a 24 mm body and stage-dependent gearhead lengths of 24.6, 30.4, 36.2 and 42.0 mm. Use the approved drawing for the complete shaft, mounting, input-interface and tolerance notation.
