# SG-6010HB Compact Planetary Joint Actuator

## Product Overview

SG-6010HB is a compact planetary joint motor published by SigGear for **exoskeleton joint** applications. The current catalog configuration combines a BLDC motor with a 9.67:1 planetary reduction stage in a 67.0 mm outer-diameter, 25.57 mm-thick package.

The exoskeleton label is the catalog application positioning for this model. It is **not a statement that the product is limited to exoskeletons**. SG-6010HB can also be evaluated for other compact joint-drive or wearable-robot mechanisms when the published torque, speed, size, voltage and load limits match the project.

## Electronics and Control Boundary

The current published SG-6010HB catalog source does **not** specify a standard integrated driver, encoder, communication protocol or connector/pinout.

Therefore the following must be confirmed for the ordered configuration:

- Driver configuration
- Encoder / feedback configuration
- CAN, RS485 or other communication requirement
- Position / velocity / torque-control requirement
- Connector and cable arrangement
- Current limits and firmware
- Brake or power-off holding requirement

Do not assume that SG-6010C or SG-6010D electronics automatically apply to SG-6010HB.

## Exoskeleton and Wearable-Robot Evaluation

The SigGear catalog explicitly identifies SG-6010HB as an **exoskeleton joint motor**.

For an exoskeleton or wearable robot project, evaluate at least:

- Joint position: hip, knee, ankle, shoulder, elbow or auxiliary axis
- Continuous assistance torque
- Peak torque and allowed peak duration
- Required output speed and range of motion
- Maximum joint diameter, thickness and mass
- Radial / axial / overturning load path
- Backdrivability or holding behavior
- Driver, encoder and communication architecture
- Duty cycle, ambient temperature and heat path
- Power-off safety strategy

## Other Potential Joint Applications

SG-6010HB is not restricted to exoskeletons. The same compact planetary motor-reducer architecture may be evaluated for:

- Rehabilitation and wearable-assistance mechanisms
- Compact robotic joints
- Research robot joints
- Low-profile actuator modules
- Custom human-assist mechanisms

Suitability for any application must be confirmed from the actual torque-speed cycle, packaging, load direction and control requirements.
