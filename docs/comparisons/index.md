# Gearbox and Robot Joint Comparison Guides

SigGear comparison guides help robotics and automation teams compare transmission architectures before selecting a product family. These pages focus on engineering trade-offs rather than declaring one technology universally better.

Final selection should be based on the complete torque-speed cycle, reduction ratio, installation envelope, weight, backlash, stiffness, external loads, duty cycle, thermal conditions, control architecture, prototype plan and production quantity.

## Published Comparison Guides

### Planetary vs Cycloidal Gearbox

Compare planetary and cycloidal transmissions by torque, output speed, reduction ratio, backlash, packaging, shock load, motor integration and application type.

### Cycloidal vs Harmonic Drive

Compare cycloidal and strain-wave, commonly called harmonic-drive, architectures for robot joints, including torque density, ratio, backlash, shock load, bearing support, service life and packaging.
