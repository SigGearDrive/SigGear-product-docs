# Electric Pruning Shears: Planetary Gearhead and Motor Selection

A cordless pruning shear needs a high-force blade stroke in a small hand-held housing. A motor + planetary gearhead can be evaluated, but the **blade cutting force is not automatically the gearhead output torque**.

## Calculate from the blade back to the gearbox

At the cutting point, the required blade moment is approximately **T_blade = F_cut × r_perpendicular**. For a crank, gear sector or lead screw, convert that moment through the *actual transmission geometry and efficiency* to the reducer output shaft. Check normal cuts separately from a jammed or blocked blade; an advertised momentary-torque limit is **not** a continuous duty rating.

## What the OEM Engineer Must Check

| Item | Required input or validation |
| --- | --- |
| **Cutting stroke** | Cutting force versus blade angle, leverage, closing time and branch load range |
| **Motor** | Loaded motor rpm, supply voltage, current limit and thermal protection |
| **Packaging** | Available motor + gearhead length, shaft coupling, radial load and enclosure sealing |
| **Safety** | Trigger release, jam recovery and prevention of unexpected blade restart |

**Supply options:** Gearhead Only for a suitable existing motor, or a Motor + Gearhead Assembly after interface review. [Motor-only technical sheets](../engineering-resources/motors/index.md) are available separately.

**Boundary:** These are **published frame sizes to compare**, not validated pruning-shear drives. Outdoor splash resistance and application endurance are not established by the generic PDF.
