**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

**Application:** The gearbox sees a blade-mechanism load, not simply the force measured at the branch. Include cutting peaks and a blocked blade.

**Engineering checks:** blade linkage torque and cutting stroke; peak stall versus continuous rated load; jam protection and environmental sealing.
