# Humanoid Robot Joint Actuators & Gearheads

**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

[**Motors · Datasheets**](../engineering-resources/motors/index.md){ .md-button }

[**Motor + Gearhead Assemblies**](../custom-engineering/motor-gearbox-integration.md){ .md-button }

**Application:** Humanoid joint design depends on peak and continuous torque, inertia and control. SigGear's planetary gearheads can be evaluated for compact auxiliary axes; larger integrated joints use separate actuator products.

**Engineering checks:** joint duty-cycle torque and speed; backdrivability, encoder and brake; gearhead bearing loads and thermal margin.
