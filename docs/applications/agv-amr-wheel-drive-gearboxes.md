**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

**Application:** A small robot may use geared motion for wheel drive or a lift, hatch or sensor axis. Wheel-drive suitability requires traction, payload and bearing checks.

**Engineering checks:** wheel radius and traction torque; continuous drive duty and bearing support; braking and safe stopping.
