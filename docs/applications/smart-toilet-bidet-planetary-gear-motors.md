**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

**Application:** A motorized toilet lid is a hinge-moment problem, a bidet nozzle is a linear travel-force problem, and a water diverter is a rotary end-stop problem. A gearhead-sized enclosure is not a waterproof drive.

**Engineering checks:** Check worst-case lid torque through the opening angle, nozzle force and stroke, quiet travel, pinch/jam protection and the final assembly's moisture barrier.
