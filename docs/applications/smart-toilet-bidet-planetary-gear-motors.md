# Smart Toilet and Bidet Gear Motors: Automatic Lid and Nozzle Drives

An automatic toilet lid, a retractable bidet nozzle and a water diverter are **three different mechanical jobs**. Miniature DC motors and planetary gearheads may be evaluated for selected motions, but none of these published gearheads is a certified complete smart-toilet actuator.

## Sizing a powered lid or nozzle

For a lid hinge, calculate the angle-dependent gravitational moment from **mass × gravity × centre-of-mass lever arm**, plus friction and acceleration. A nozzle extension uses rack/screw friction and linear force instead. For both, check short obstruction loads, a safe manual release and any position sensor; do not assume reduction alone holds the lid when unpowered.

## What the OEM Engineer Must Check

| Item | Required input or validation |
| --- | --- |
| **Lid or seat** | Hinge torque through travel, safe closing speed, soft stop and pinch detection |
| **Nozzle or diverter** | Stroke, linear force or valve torque, sealing and jam detection |
| **Wet environment** | Condensation, cleaning agents and the separate enclosure's protection rating |
| **Motor matching** | Supply voltage, current limit, available space, shaft fit and lifecycle testing |

**Supply options:** Gearhead Only for a suitable existing motor, or a Motor + Gearhead Assembly after interface review. [Motor-only technical sheets](../engineering-resources/motors/index.md) are available separately.

**Boundary:** The published gearhead files do not certify moisture resistance, toilet safety compliance or direct water contact. The finished product requires OEM validation.
