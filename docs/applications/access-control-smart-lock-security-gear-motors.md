# Smart Lock & Electronic Latch Gear Motors

**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

[**Motors · Datasheets**](../engineering-resources/motors/index.md){ .md-button }

**Application:** A smart door lock drives a bolt or cam against seal friction, spring force and possible door misalignment. The loaded torque may be very different from the free-running latch motion.

**Engineering checks:** Start with bolt force × effective cam radius, then check cold/low-battery starting, jam current, manual override and emergency-egress requirements. Standard gearhead files do not certify a lock.
