# Laboratory Sample Carousel & Positioner Gear Motors

**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

[**Motors · Datasheets**](../engineering-resources/motors/index.md){ .md-button }

**Application:** A reagent carousel indexes discrete sample positions, while a pipette or sample-handling carriage travels along a belt or lead screw. They require different motion, loading and contamination checks.

**Engineering checks:** Specify index pitch or carriage stroke, speed and settling time, required feedback, blocked-motion force, splash boundary and service life for the full instrument.
