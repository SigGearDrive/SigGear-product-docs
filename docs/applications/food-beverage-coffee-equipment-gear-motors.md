# Coffee Machine & Dosing Gear Motors

**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

**Application:** Coffee dosing augers, grinder adjusters and beverage valves have different loads. Blocked grounds and hygiene boundaries must be considered.

**Engineering checks:** auger flow per turn and jam torque; grinder adjustment backlash; food-contact separation and cleaning.
