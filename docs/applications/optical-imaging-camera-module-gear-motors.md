# Camera Focus, Zoom & Optical Gear Motors

**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

[**Motors · Datasheets**](../engineering-resources/motors/index.md){ .md-button }

**Application:** Lens focus, filter-wheel and sensor positioning mechanisms have different backlash and vibration tolerances; some may favour direct drive over a gearbox.

**Engineering checks:** fine positioning and backlash; image vibration and acoustic noise; envelope, feedback and motor coupling.
