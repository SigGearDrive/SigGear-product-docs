**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

**Application:** A camera focus barrel needs small controlled motion; zoom-ring and sensor-adjustment mechanisms have different travel and reversal requirements. Adding a gearhead can supply torque but also introduces backlash and vibration.

**Engineering checks:** Determine required focus travel or zoom rotation, reversal deadband, lens-settling vibration, motor feedback and the available shaft/mounting envelope. For very light axes, compare direct drive before choosing reduction.
