# Medical Equipment Auxiliary Micro Gearheads

**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

[**Motors · Datasheets**](../engineering-resources/motors/index.md){ .md-button }

**Application:** For a shielded, non-patient-contact adjustment axis inside medical equipment, a small motor and gearhead may provide controlled auxiliary motion. The standard gearhead is not a sterile, patient-contact or medical-certified device.

**Engineering checks:** Define the auxiliary load, travel and stall limit; evaluate heat, acoustic noise, cleaning boundary, traceability and device-level electrical/mechanical risk controls.
