**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

**Application:** Pan/tilt lighting axes and small signage actuators require different backlash, duty-cycle and cable-management constraints.

**Engineering checks:** pan/tilt speed and driven inertia; backlash, noise and end stops; cable routing and heat.
