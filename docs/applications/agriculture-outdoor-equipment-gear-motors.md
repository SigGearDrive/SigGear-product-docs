**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

**Application:** Outdoor seed meters and irrigation valves need intermittent low-speed motion but face dust, rain and variable loads.

**Engineering checks:** metering rate and screw or rotary torque; dirt and blocked-drive behavior; sealing and outdoor duty.
