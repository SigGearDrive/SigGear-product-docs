# HVAC Damper & Smart Vent Gear Motors

**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

[**Motors · Datasheets**](../engineering-resources/motors/index.md){ .md-button }

**Application:** Small dampers and ventilation flaps rotate through limited angles under seal friction and variable airflow forces.

**Engineering checks:** damper torque and motion angle; end-stop, stall and power-off behavior; temperature, moisture and noise.
