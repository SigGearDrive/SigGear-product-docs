**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

**Application:** A small air damper rotates through a limited angle against seal contact and air pressure. The motor, feedback and fail-safe mechanism determine how the flap stops or returns when power is lost.

**Engineering checks:** Measure breakaway torque and full-stroke angle; check end-stop/stall behavior, motor duty, noise and the required unpowered position. Fire/smoke damper approvals are separate from gearhead selection.
