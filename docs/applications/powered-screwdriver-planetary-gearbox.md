# Planetary Gearbox for Powered Screwdrivers and Fastening Tools

Electric screwdrivers and compact fastening tools often use a high-speed DC or BLDC motor with a reduction gearbox to obtain controlled output speed and higher fastening torque.

SigGear's **8-42 mm planetary gearbox series** can be evaluated for precision screwdrivers, compact cordless tools and higher-torque fastening mechanisms. Final selection depends on the fastening torque, speed, duty cycle, motor and output interface.

## Application Segments

### Precision Electric Screwdrivers

Small electronics and instrument assembly tools may prioritize:

- small diameter
- low weight
- controlled output speed
- smooth low-speed behavior
- repeatable fastening
- low vibration and noise

Smaller gearbox families can be evaluated when the required torque and motor power are within the published limits.

### Compact Cordless Screwdrivers

Consumer and professional compact tools may require a balance of:

- torque
- packaging diameter
- battery efficiency
- intermittent duty
- impact / jam tolerance
- cost and manufacturing scalability

### Higher-Torque Fastening Tools

Larger frame sizes may be more appropriate where the normal working torque, peak torque or motor power exceeds the capability of smaller gearboxes.

## Preliminary Frame Orientation

| Frame group | Typical fastening-tool direction |
| --- | --- |
| **10P-20P** | miniature / precision powered screwdrivers and compact mechanisms |
| **20P-28P** | compact cordless fastening tools and moderate-torque applications |
| **32P-42P** | higher-torque fastening tools, industrial compact actuators and larger motors |

This table is only a starting point. The exact ratio, stage count and torque rating must be confirmed from the selected gearbox model.

## Information Needed for Selection

Please provide:

- fastening torque
- target output rpm
- motor model and motor speed
- battery / supply voltage
- allowable outside diameter
- allowable gearbox length
- output shaft or chuck interface
- normal duty cycle
- stall / jam requirement
- backlash or repeatability target
- noise target
- prototype and annual quantity

## Torque Control Boundary

The gearbox provides the mechanical reduction. Final screw torque repeatability also depends on the motor, driver, current / torque control, clutch strategy, output tooling and fastening process.

For projects with strict tightening accuracy requirements, define the complete control and measurement method before production release.
