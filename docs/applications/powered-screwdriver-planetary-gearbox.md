# Electric Screwdriver Planetary Gearhead & Motors

**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

[**Motors · Datasheets**](../engineering-resources/motors/index.md){ .md-button }

**Application:** A high-speed DC/BLDC motor with reduction can drive small screws, but stall and impact loads at the fastener must be assessed separately.

**Engineering checks:** fastener torque and loaded rpm; peak tightening or jam event; bit holder, shaft coupling and duty.
