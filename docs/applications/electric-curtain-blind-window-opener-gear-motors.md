**Application:** A curtain rail needs belt pull at its pulley radius; a roller blind has a changing winding radius.

**Check:** Check loaded speed, noise, end stops and power-off holding; a planetary gearhead is not self-locking.

*Candidate sizes only. Rated torque and length depend on the selected stage/ratio; verify motor coupling and mechanical interface. Reduction does not guarantee holding.*
