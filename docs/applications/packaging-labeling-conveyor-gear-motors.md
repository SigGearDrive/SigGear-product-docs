**Application:** A label-feeder roller needs steady web transport and registration; a miniature conveyor also carries belt or product traction loads. Roller drive torque depends on line pull and effective roller radius.

**Engineering checks:** Start with required line pull × roller radius, then verify continuous output rpm, acceleration peaks, slip, and separate radial bearing support for the driven roller.
