# Automotive Small-Motion Gear Motors

**SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.**

**Application:** Interior flaps, vents and camera covers require short strokes and compact assemblies; vehicle temperatures and qualification are system-specific.

**Engineering checks:** motion angle and stall/end-stop load; automotive temperature and power transients; vehicle electrical and qualification requirements.
