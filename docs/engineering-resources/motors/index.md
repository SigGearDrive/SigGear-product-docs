<summary>Engineering notes & source-document limitations</summary>

### Ø8 mm

Original source drawings and charts are retained; motor-mating and operating conditions require engineering review.

### Ø10 mm

Draft source notation for inductance (*Mh*) and some current/speed/inertia fields requires confirmation before final selection.

### Ø16 mm

Draft sheets include disputed 120132 winding data and several source records where rated torque equals stall torque. Do not treat those as confirmed continuous ratings.

### Ø20 mm

HR-BL2030-Q120260 is a motor-only 12 V BLDC datasheet. The quoted 30.4 mN·m locked-rotor torque is not a continuous rating.

### Ø22 mm

Sheets 02 and 03 share model codes but differ in mechanical drawings and report different speeds. Confirm the exact drawing; Sheet 06 no-load-current notation also needs checking.

### Ø24 mm

HR-BL2453-Q240150100-12-55 is a motor-only 24 V BLDC datasheet. Locked-rotor torque is not a continuous rating.

### Ø28 mm

Draft A/B sheets represent different motor groups. Charts and tables disagree for Sheet A; Sheet B contains an unusual +155 °C ambient source value requiring confirmation.

### Ø32 mm

Series 02 Mechanical A and B share electrical codes but have different outlines. Original chart/drawing raster quality and unusual Series 01 bearing-load values need engineering validation.
