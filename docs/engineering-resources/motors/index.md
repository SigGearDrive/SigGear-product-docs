<details class="siggear-motor-engineering-notes" markdown="1">
<summary>Engineering notes & source-document limitations</summary>

### Ø8 mm

Original source drawings and charts are retained; motor-mating and operating conditions require engineering review.

### Ø10 mm

**New Draft V1.1 sheets:** Series 01, 03 and 04 now contain vector-retypeset parameter tables and larger source drawing/curve references. Series 02 remains a separate earlier motor group. The HS-1020-Q source group has ambiguous model identifiers and one speed inconsistency; engineering verification is mandatory. The new Series 04 source labels (015126 / 045126) differ from the older directory labels (015124 / 045124). Inductance (*Mh*) and other unusual source values still require review. **Original curves remain low-resolution rasters**; reformatting does not restore measured accuracy. Historical Draft V1.0 PDF URLs remain accessible.

### Ø16 mm

Draft sheets include disputed 120132 winding data and source values where rated torque equals stall torque. Original diagram images are roughly 63–89 DPI and are not controlled dimensional drawings.

### Ø20 mm

HR-BL2030-Q120260 is a motor-only 12 V BLDC datasheet. The quoted 30.4 mN·m locked-rotor torque is not a continuous rating. New DC motor sources **120108** and **120100** are distinct from that BLDC motor. The 120108 screenshot contains a chart labeled **HS-2016-Q**; its correspondence requires engineering confirmation. These 2 new files are **Draft V1.0**.

### Ø22 mm

Sheets 02 and 03 share model codes but differ in mechanical drawings and report different speeds. Confirm the exact drawing; Sheet 06 no-load-current notation also needs checking.

### Ø24 mm

HR-BL2453-Q240150100-12-55 is a motor-only 24 V BLDC datasheet. Locked-rotor torque is not a continuous rating. The new DC family **090080 / 120074 / 150082 / 180088 / 240092** is separate and provides 5 source winding options (9–24 V); bearing alternatives and original curve/dimensions require review. **Draft V1.0**.

### Ø28 mm

Draft A/B sheets represent different motor groups. Sheet A source chart and table disagree; Sheet B includes an unusual +155 °C source limit and a roughly 115 DPI chart, both requiring review.

### Ø32 mm

Series 02 Mechanical A and B share electrical codes but have different outlines. Original diagram/curve source images are roughly 72–73 DPI; original drawing dimensions and unusual Series 01 bearing-load values require controlled engineering review.
