# About SigGear

Guangdong SigGear Drive Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. specializes in compact precision gear transmission products and engineering support for robotics, smart hardware, automation and other products that require small motors, gearboxes or integrated drive solutions.

## Engineering and Project Support

For applicable projects, SigGear can start from a customer's concept, drawing, existing motor or installation constraints and review the transmission approach with the engineering team.

The review may include motor, reducer, encoder and driver integration, mechanical interfaces, reduction ratios, output shafts, housings, cable arrangements and other configuration details supported by the selected product family.

Prototype manufacturing, testing and design refinement can be used where required before the configuration is prepared for production. Final feasibility and specifications depend on the application requirements, selected structure, quantity and engineering review.
