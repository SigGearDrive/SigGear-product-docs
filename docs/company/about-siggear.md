# About SigGear

Guangdong SigGear Drive Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. specializes in precision transmission products for robotics and automation.

## Product Focus

SigGear's public product documentation covers:

- Integrated robot joint actuators
- Cycloidal reducers and joint modules
- Planetary gearboxes
- Hub gear motors

Product specifications are published by model. Final suitability depends on the selected configuration and application requirements.

## OEM and ODM Customization

SigGear provides OEM and ODM customization services for precision transmission projects. The confirmed customization scope can include:

- Mechanical customization
- Size customization
- Logo customization
- Packaging customization

Mechanical and dimensional changes are reviewed against the application requirements and the approved configuration before quotation or production.

## Manufacturing Capability

SigGear operates its own production lines covering:

- Precision machining
- Assembly
- Testing

Public product pages list only verified technical data. Project-specific dimensions, interfaces and performance requirements are confirmed during technical review.

## Information for a Custom Project

To evaluate an OEM or ODM request, please provide the information available for your project, such as:

- Application and product type
- Required torque and output speed
- Size and mounting limits
- Motor voltage and power requirements, if applicable
- Backlash target, if relevant
- Duty cycle
- Prototype and estimated annual quantity
- Drawings or interface requirements, if available

This information helps SigGear match an existing product or determine whether mechanical customization is required.
