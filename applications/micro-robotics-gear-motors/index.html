    Micro Robotics Gear Motors & Planetary Reducers¶

    SigGear is an OEM supplier of DC/BLDC motors, precision planetary gearheads (Ø8–42 mm), and motor + gearhead assemblies.

    Motors · Datasheets Gearheads · PDF / 2D / STEP Motor + Gearhead Assemblies

    Application: Small robots and grippers often need more output torque than a bare high-speed motor provides, with limited space for both motor and gearbox.

    Engineering checks: package diameter and total length; output torque at loaded motor speed; feedback and shaft-bearing support.
