SigGear's compact planetary gearbox series covers 12 frame sizes from 8 mm to 42 mm, for preliminary selection in space-constrained OEM mechanisms.

Micro (8–16 mm), Miniature (20–32 mm), and Compact (36–42 mm) planetary gearboxes can be reviewed as gearbox-only components or as part of a motor + gearbox engineering project. Each published family has a datasheet, mechanical-interface reference and a simplified four-stage STEP model for preliminary packaging. Final stage, ratio, torque, shaft and motor compatibility require configuration review.
