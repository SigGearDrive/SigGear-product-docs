### Long company description

Guangdong SigGear Drive Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. (SigGear) develops and manufactures compact precision transmission solutions for OEM engineering programs.

SigGear's core capability is custom transmission engineering that can move from concept or an existing design into repeatable manufacturing. Projects may begin from an existing customer motor, CAD or mechanical drawing, a gearbox that is failing in service, a special ratio / shaft / flange / housing requirement, a robot joint concept, or a prototype program expected to move into volume production.

Engineering review may cover planetary or cycloidal transmission architecture, motor integration, reduction ratio, input and output interfaces, packaging, bearing / load arrangement, encoder / driver / communication configuration where applicable, prototype validation and production configuration.

The company publishes standard 8–42 mm planetary gearheads, planetary robot joint actuators and cycloidal joint modules with datasheets, drawings and STEP CAD where released. These standard products are public reference platforms. Many customer-specific products and production programs cannot be shown publicly because they are protected by confidentiality agreements.

SigGear's real manufacturing environment includes precision machining, gear processing, assembly, quality inspection, metrology and testing activities. For applicable customer programs, the development path can continue from prototype and design freeze into pilot preparation, repeatable batch manufacturing and production delivery planning.

Actual specifications, production quantity and lead time depend on the exact configuration, materials, tooling, inspection requirements and schedule.
