**First four:** 10P (micro), 20P (miniature), 32P (miniature + Design-In), 42P (compact + Design-In). Together they cover the three planned small-diameter product groups. All four have an identifiable real-product photograph, technical datasheet, mechanical-interface information and a released public simplified four-stage STEP. 32P and 42P have dedicated Design-In and motor-integration guidance.

**Requested DirectIndustry taxonomy:** primary **Planetary Gear Reducer** (https://www.directindustry.com/industrial-manufacturer/planetary-gear-reducer-68955.html). Request optional relevant discovery tags/categories, conditional on DirectIndustry's classification policy: **Planetary Micro-Gearbox** (10P), **Miniature Gear Reducer** (20P and 32P), **Compact Gear Reducer** (42P). These public search categories are not confirmation that a paid subscription includes multi-category placement.

**Release control:** publish one listing per existing family reference. Do not present the family as one orderable ratio/stage-specific SKU. The below ranges are taken verbatim from existing public SigGear controlled inventory and span different configurations. Do not claim all range endpoints apply simultaneously. Avoid zero-backlash, maintenance-free, guaranteed life, low-noise measurements and confirmed customer applications without evidence.

**Unit caveat:** the source inventory expresses allowable torque in `kgf·cm`, whereas DirectIndustry sample listings commonly render torque in `N·m`. Keep source unit values in this editorial draft. Have engineering approve any Nm conversions and associated stage selection before filling a structured site torque field. The maximum momentary torque cannot be entered as continuous/rated torque.

**Full description (editable publication draft):**

The SigGear 10P is a 10 mm micro planetary gearbox (planetary gearhead) for space-constrained OEM mechanisms. Published configurations cover one to four reduction stages, with different ratios and gearbox body lengths. SigGear can evaluate gearbox-only supply for an existing motor or review a motor-matched assembly using the customer's shaft, speed, load and available space. A public datasheet, mechanical-interface reference and simplified four-stage STEP are available for preliminary mechanical design. Final dimensions, torque and motor compatibility must be confirmed for the selected configuration.
