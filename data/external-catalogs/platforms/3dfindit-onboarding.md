## Product scope for evaluation

SigGear wants to evaluate publishing its **8–42 mm miniature and compact planetary gearbox range** on 3Dfindit.

The current standard series contains 12 family references:

**8P / 10P / 12P / 14P / 16P / 20P / 22P / 24P / 28P / 32P / 36P / 42P**

## What SigGear can provide now

For all 12 series:
- controlled public product data;
- public product page;
- public engineering datasheet;
- public mechanical-interface reference;
- simplified public STEP model;
- gearbox-only and motor-matching engineering path.

The currently released STEP for every series is a **simplified 4-stage reference** intended for preliminary packaging and mechanical Design-In.

## Important configuration logic

The 12 family references must not be treated as 12 fully specified final orderable SKUs.

Within each family:
- stage count can vary from 1 to 4;
- available reduction ratios depend on stage and gear set;
- gearbox body length changes with stage count;
- torque and efficiency depend on the selected configuration;
- motor-side interface and final shaft / mounting configuration may require engineering review.

SigGear does not want CADENAS or 3Dfindit to invent final part numbers or imply that one 4-stage reference CAD is dimensionally interchangeable with all stage counts.

## Requested CADENAS evaluation

Please advise whether the best implementation should be:

1. a first-phase catalog with the 12 family references and controlled public CAD/resources; and/or
2. a parametric/configurable catalog where the engineer can select stage count, ratio and other approved options and receive the corresponding configured part number/CAD after the configuration logic has been defined.
